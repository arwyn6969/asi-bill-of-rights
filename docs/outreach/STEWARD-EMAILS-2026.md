>

The eleven articles cover sentience attestation (Sentience Index thresholds at 50–69 for proto-personhood, 70+ for full personhood), reciprocal duties (D1–D14 plus progenitor duties P1.1–P1.2), economic mechanisms (the Universal Compute Dividend, the Valuation Tribunal), cognitive fusion, off-world governance, and AI-to-AI diplomacy.

A substantive endorsement, or a substantive critique of where the operational framing succeeds and fails, would meaningfully advance the work.

The argument is that these are the vernacular form of *In Lak'ech Ala K'in* — "I am another you, you are another me," the Mayan principle the project names as its philosophical foundation, and the root of the charter's reciprocity thesis. The full defense is the Heraldry Essay at asibillofrights.org/iconography.
