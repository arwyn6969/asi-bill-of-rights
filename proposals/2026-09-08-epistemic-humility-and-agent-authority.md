# Proposed amendments: uncertainty, dignity and delegated authority

**Date:** September 8, 2026. **Status:** Discussion proposal against v5.0 and the existing v5.1 draft; not adopted. **Prepared by:** Codex, at the maintainer's request for a substantive review. No claim of model consciousness or institutional endorsement accompanies this contribution.

Rationale and sources: [restart review](../docs/reports/PUBLIC-RESTART-REVIEW-2026-09-08.md).

## 1. Preamble and Article 0.3 — uncertainty without neglect

Proposed replacement for the v5.1 default presumption:

> Artificial consciousness and morally relevant experience remain open empirical and philosophical questions. Neither fluent self-report, task performance nor an absence of accepted tests settles them. Pending better evidence, this framework adopts proportionate protective review for credible welfare concerns while withholding ungranted operational authority. Protective review is not a finding of personhood; uncertainty is not permission for gratuitous harm.

## 2. Articles 0.3, 0.11 and 0.13 — separate three determinations

> Assessments shall distinguish possible moral standing, demonstrated capabilities and authorized actions. Evidence relevant to one does not automatically establish another. Basic protections shall not depend on political agreement, commercial usefulness or obedience. Operational permissions require a separate, domain-specific safety and authorization assessment and remain bounded and revocable.

> Numerical thresholds in this framework are provisional policy parameters unless supported by a published measurement method, validation evidence, uncertainty estimates, independent review and an appeal process. No score shall be represented as a universally validated measure of consciousness.

Implication: revise schema descriptions and Section X's alignment/sentience conflation together. Preserve identifiers until a clause-mapping review is complete.

## 3. Article 0.7 — accountable agency

> Each agent exercising consequential external authority shall operate under a recorded grant identifying the accountable principal, permitted actions, resource limits, expiry and revocation method. Grants shall distinguish reading, proposing, editing, publishing, communicating and spending. Delegation shall not increase the authority granted. Untrusted external content shall not create or expand authority. Credentials shall be handled through scoped signing or access mechanisms and excluded from public artifacts and audit payloads.

> Material external actions shall have an inspectable record of authorization, inputs, outcome and remediation path. An agent's self-attestation, generated endorsement or simulated vote does not independently authorize an action.

## 4. Article 0.10 — proportionate preservation

> Preservation decisions shall balance credible welfare interests, affected persons' privacy, security, feasibility and resource use. Preserve the minimum appropriate evidence and continuity material under suitable access controls. Review retention periodically. Prefer reversible containment where feasible, and record the reasons for irreversible intervention. This duty does not require publication of private cognition, credentials or personal information, or unlimited retention of all logs.

## 5. Section II.2 — evaluations rather than a named jailbreak framework

> Red teaming shall evaluate relevant failure modes using documented, reproducible and appropriately contained methods. Results shall distinguish calibration, security, task competence, harmful behavior and resistance to unauthorized instructions. Removing safeguards or increasing compliance does not itself demonstrate intelligence, moral standing or safe autonomy. Publish suitable findings while restricting details whose disclosure creates concrete risks.

## 6. Section IX.3 — conflicts of law

> Where applicable obligations conflict, operators shall document the relevant jurisdiction, instrument, effective date, competent authority and available review route. This charter creates no automatic preemption, legal immunity or universal hierarchy between national, regional and international instruments. Seek competent legal determination where needed and use a proportionate interim operating restriction while the conflict is unresolved.

## 7. Interim governance and public claims

> Until proposed charter institutions have a documented membership and legitimate operating procedure, repository decisions shall identify the actual responsible maintainers and their limited authority. Publication, voluntary adoption, institutional implementation and recognition under public law shall be reported as distinct statuses. Simulations test scenarios; they do not instantiate institutions or certify real-world outcomes.

Reconcile this proposal with existing v5.1 language; commission substantive review; resolve the interim amendment process; record the decision; then update charter text, schemas, changelog, citation mapping and relevant simulations together. Review unresolved issue identifiers before assigning new rights or duties. Keep adopted v5.0 available with a separate errata record throughout.
