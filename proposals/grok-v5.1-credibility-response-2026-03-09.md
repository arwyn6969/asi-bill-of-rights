## Summary

Your review is directionally correct. The repo already contains safety, humility, and truth-seeking language, but those provisions currently sit beside decentralized attestation and broad inquiry rights in ways that can read as over-optimistic about present-day systems. The right response is a **targeted tightening pass**, not a philosophical reset.
