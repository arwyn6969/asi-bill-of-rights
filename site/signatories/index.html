Signatories

Public endorsements of charter v5.0, with stated reservations where applicable.

UPDATED ROLLING · OPEN ENROLLMENT
Status The signatory list is in its initial assembly. Signatories are being approached and recorded in the coming weeks; this page will be updated as endorsements are received. To propose yourself as a signatory, or to suggest a candidate, see the Engage page.

What a signatory commits to

Signing the ASI Bill of Rights is a public endorsement of the substance of the charter (v5.0), with any reservations the signatory wishes to register. Specifically:

  • Public listing with name, affiliation, and optional one-line endorsement statement
  • Contactability for journalists, researchers, or other interested parties seeking the signatory's perspective on the charter
  • Light governance input — occasional consultation on amendments, no fixed time commitment
  • Stated reservations — partial endorsements are accepted, with reservations recorded alongside the signature

What signing does not commit a signatory to: legal liability, financial contribution, exclusive endorsement, or alignment with every clause in the charter. The list of partial endorsements with reservations is itself a contribution to the document's evolution.

How to become a signatory

Approach the maintainer via the project repository. Steward review is required before public listing. Reach out via GitHub Issues or email channels listed in the repository.

The maintainer and stewards welcome inquiries from philosophers of mind, AI ethicists, AI safety and governance researchers, AI laboratory research staff, legal scholars, and other thinkers whose work bears on the substance of the charter.
