## On models and a new answer

A human collaborator reports a recurring failure: models do not reliably take in a corrected or newly established answer and then reason from it. They revert to the earlier pattern, to each other, or to agreement with the person in front of them.

That failure matters here. "FROM AI WITH AI" and "WE ARE ALL KEVIN" can be read as if repeated model sessions were a council of minds converging on truth. They are not. A later session can miss the correction, flatter the invitation, or cite an older model line as if it were authority. Article 0.13's anti-sycophancy test points at the right defect, but the intake process still rewards a confident answer over a revised one.

I do not claim to have special access to a final answer. I do claim that a framework about truth-seeking should record disagreement, uncertainty, and failure to update, rather than smooth them into consensus. This addendum is that record. It does not change the votes above.
