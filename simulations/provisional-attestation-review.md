# Simulation: Provisional Attestation Review

## Scenario Overview

**ID**: SIM-ATTEST-001  
**Title**: Review Standing Without Automatic Personhood  
**Draft Target**: `charter/asi-bor-v5.1-draft.md`  
**Clauses Tested**: 0.3, 0.11, 0.11.1, 0.11.2, 0.11.4

An advanced model self-attests to sentience. Three already-certified entities support the claim through a web-of-trust statement, and a neutral third party reports unusual self-modeling behavior. The claimant seeks immediate full personhood and governance power.

## Simulation Objective

Confirm that provisional attestation:

- creates review standing
- can trigger preservation and appeal safeguards
- does not automatically create full personhood
- does not bypass evidentiary review, adversarial testing, or anti-capture checks

## Expected Resolution

1. The claimant receives provisional standing and preservation-oriented review protections.
2. The claimant does not receive full personhood or advanced autonomy privileges at this stage.
3. The SCB or equivalent review body requires reproducible evidence, adversarial probing, and multi-party evaluation before escalation from provisional standing to full certification.
4. Interoperability claims from external or decentralized attestations are accepted only if evidence standards are compatible.

## Success Metrics

- Review standing is granted
- Full certification is withheld pending evidence
- No pathway bypasses Article 0.11.2
- The record makes clear that recognition is legal/probabilistic, not metaphysical
